Most summer camps treat goalkeepers as an afterthought. At the Pro GK Residential Experience, they're the whole point: one week living, training and recovering like professional goalkeepers, with Pro GK Academy staff connected to MLS clubs, national team programs, European clubs and D1 college programs. The camp is powered by Pro GK Academy FL and Athletum FC.

What they work on

TechnicalHandling, footwork and shot-stopping fundamentals
TacticalDecision-making, positioning and game scenarios
PhysicalMovement efficiency, power and injury prevention
PsychologicalFocus, resilience and composure under pressure
SocialCommunication, leadership and team dynamics

Where they live and train

Great Wolf Lodge, with indoor water park and group suites, and Paradise Coast Sports Complex, home of FC Naples, with professional-grade fields and an on-site strength and performance gym. All meals are included and guided by a nutrition specialist.
